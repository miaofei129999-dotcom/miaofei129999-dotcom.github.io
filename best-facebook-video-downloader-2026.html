The four things that decide whether a tool is usable

  1. It handles Reels, not just feed videos. Reels are now the majority of what people want to save, and they load through a different path than a standard video post. Tools that only support the old feed format fail on most of what is actually shared.
  2. It does not require an account. The moment a downloader asks you to sign in, you have handed your session to a third party for a single file. Public video is public; saving it should not require a login.
  3. It offers the quality that exists. Plenty of pages advertise 4K for a clip that was uploaded at 720p. A tool that reflects what the post actually contains is more useful than one that promises a number.
  4. It gets out of the way. Link in, file out. Redirect chains, popunders, and a required "step 1 of 3" flow are the fastest way to tell that the tool is monetizing you rather than serving you.
