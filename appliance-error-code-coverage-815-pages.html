815 Appliance Pages, Two Error-Code Lists: A Coverage Ledger

A reference page, not an essay. This is the coverage count for our appliance database, published because the numbers are worse than our own marketing implies.

Why this page exists

A reader asked for a Maytag dryer fault code and got a page about twelve washing machines, one of which had a code list. Neither matched. We counted the whole catalogue afterwards to find out how often that happens.

Method

Read sitemap-models.xml (4,800 URLs), kept the 817 brand × appliance index pages, parsed the self-tally each page prints — models documented, replacement part numbers, models with a confirmed error-code list. One curl request per page through a proxy, regex parse, 8 November 2026.

815 of 817 parsed. Two excluded rather than estimated.

Read it this way

Two error-code pages in 815 is not a coverage gap. It is a missing feature with a title tag in front of it.

The parts column is real in one category and decorative in the others. Refrigerators carry 41% of all part numbers on 30.6% of pages. Air purifiers carry none on 66 pages.

Six thousand three hundred and sixty-nine models have no part number anywhere on their category page.

Not measured

Model-level pages (3,672 of them) were not crawled, so a dash here may not reflect the model page itself.

Part numbers were not checked against manufacturer catalogues. Presence only.

Brand-to-model ratios across 503 brands were not investigated.

Corrections

The parts copy said "part numbers with the symptom each fixes". No symptom data exists. Removing it.

An internal note said "8,450 appliances with manuals". 8,450 is model records; manual links were not counted.

This ledger covers ModelNumberFinder, which we operate. Method above, misses above. No sponsor, no manufacturer preview.

Unresolved

Washers before dryers, because the 44 washing-machine pages already have 664 part numbers to hang codes on. The mail is all dryers.
