X Ranked by Repost Rate Looks Nothing Like X Ranked by Views

The same posts, sorted twice, barely overlapping.

Take the same recent set of posts on X and sort it twice: once by views, once by repost rate. The two orderings barely overlap. View counts reward accounts that already hold an audience; repost rate rewards posts that earned a second audience.

The repost-rate view, entry by entry.

  • ESPN. High views, middling reposts. Broadcast, not spread.
  • ESPN, top by views. Views carry it. Reposts do not.
  • ESPN, third entry. Same account, three different repost behaviours.
  • NFL. The repost rate tells a different story than the view count.
  • NFL, closing slot. Consistent spread, unspectacular views.
  • F1. A post punching above its follower count.
  • F1, second entry. Repost-driven reach rather than timeline reach.
  • NASA. Science posts get saved and forwarded, not only viewed.
  • BBC Earth. Evergreen. Reposted long after posting.
  • Discovery. The sleeper entry on this list.
  • The Weather Channel. Utility posts travel by repost.

A view-ranked feed tells you who is loud. A repost-ranked feed tells you what is worth keeping. When what you want to keep is a post someone forwarded as a thread, TweetArticleSave pulls the article view of it.
