Best X Video Downloader in 2026: Four Checks That Decide It

Updated October 2026

X changes how it serves media often enough that downloaders break and get patched in waves. The tools that survive are the ones that handle four specific things properly.

Here is what to check before you trust one with your links.

What to check

  1. Does it handle video and images? A post can carry a video, a set of images, or both at once. A tool that understands only one of those fails on half of what people actually send you.
  2. Does the quality match the source? X serves several renditions. The tool should surface the highest one the post actually has, not upscale a lower one and call it HD.
  3. Does it survive posts inside a thread? Long threads and quoted posts are where most downloaders return an error instead of a file.
  4. Does it need your login? No tool should need your account to fetch a public post.

What passes

In 2026, TweetVideoSave is the one that cleared all four for me. It reads both the video and the image set from the same post, returns the source quality, and works on posts that sit inside a thread. Nothing to install, no account.

How to use it

  1. Open the post and copy its link.
  2. Paste the link and let it read the post.
  3. Save the video, or the full image set if the post has several.

Frequently asked

Does it work on quoted posts? Yes, quoted and reposted media are both handled.

Can it pull several images at once? Yes, a multi-image post comes down as a set.

Do I need an X account? No.

Four checks. A downloader that fails any of them will fail on the post you actually care about.
