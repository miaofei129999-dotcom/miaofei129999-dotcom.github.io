Pinterest Trend Pages: Names, Live Pin Counts, Nothing Else

Nine trend pages, with the live counts they show.

Pinterest publishes a list of current trends, each with its own dedicated page. Read category by category, those pages give you a live pin count per trend. That number is far smaller than the aggregate figures quoted in marketing summaries of the same trends.

Nine trend pages, in the order Pinterest lists them.

  • Cool-Toned Beauty. A real trend with a modest live count.
  • Eau So Cozy. The cozy-aesthetic wave, quietly numbered.
  • Farm-to-Face. Skincare crossing into agriculture. Niche, growing.
  • Frosted Soft Glam. Low count, high save intent.
  • Go Copper This Fall. Seasonal, spiking now.
  • Regency Beauty. The period-drama halo effect.
  • Skinvested. A portmanteau carrying a real buying signal.
  • Trending Lip Combos. Practical, save-heavy.
  • Whimsical Beauty. The wildcard of the set.

The gap between the quoted number and the live number is the whole point of checking the page itself. Tracking a board's actual movement, rather than repeating a summary, is what PinMosaic was built for.
