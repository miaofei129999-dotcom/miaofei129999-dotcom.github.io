Billboard and Deezer Do Not Agree on What a Number One Is

Two charts, same week, different orderings.

Two charts, one week, same seven days of releases. Billboard's Hot 100 and Deezer's own chart are both legitimate rankings of what people are listening to, and they disagree on the ordering almost everywhere outside the top spots.
