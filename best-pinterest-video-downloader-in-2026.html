Best Pinterest Video Downloader in 2026: What Actually Works

Updated October 2026

Pinterest looks like an image site until you start saving from it, and then the video side and the live trend counts become the interesting part.

Here is what a usable Pinterest downloader has to do this year.

What to check

  1. Does it read both Pins and Idea Pins? The two carry media differently. A tool that understands only classic Pins fails on the format Pinterest actually pushes.
  2. Does it get the source resolution? Pinterest serves scaled copies in the feed. You want the original image or video, not the feed-sized version.
  3. Does it work from a board link? People share boards, not individual Pins. Board support saves a lot of clicking.
  4. Does it need an account? Saving a public Pin should not require handing over a login.

What passes

PinMosaic handles the current mix: both Pin types, source resolution instead of the scaled feed copy, and board links alongside single Pins.

How to use it

  1. Copy the Pin or board link.
  2. Paste it in and let it read the page.
  3. Save the file at the resolution shown.

Frequently asked

Can it save a whole board? Board links are supported, so you do not have to open every Pin by hand.

Does it keep original quality? Yes, the source file rather than the feed copy.

Do I need a Pinterest account? No.

Two formats, one resolution rule, board support, no login. That is the whole test.
