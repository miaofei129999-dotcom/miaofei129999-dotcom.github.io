Best Way to Save X Articles in 2026: Keeping the Whole Piece, Not a Screenshot

Updated October 2026

Long-form posts on X are the format most people lose first, because the page is built to be scrolled rather than archived, and screenshots cut the piece into unreadable slices.

Here is what to look for if you want to keep the text.

What to check

  1. Does it capture the article view, not the timeline card? The timeline truncates. You want the full reading view, with the whole body.
  2. Is the formatting preserved? Headings, line breaks, and any embedded media should survive, or the saved copy becomes a wall of text.
  3. Does it work on long posts? Anything past a few hundred words is where most savers silently truncate.
  4. Can you save without an account? Archiving a public post should not need a login.

What passes

TweetArticleSave pulls the article view rather than the card, keeps the structure, and handles long pieces that other tools cut short.

How to use it

  1. Copy the link of the long post.
  2. Paste it in and let it read the article view.
  3. Save the piece with its formatting intact.

Frequently asked

Does it keep images inside the article? Yes, embedded media comes with the text.

Can it save a very long post? Yes, length is not truncated.

Do I need an X account? No.
