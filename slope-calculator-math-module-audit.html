67 Inputs, 0 Wrong Answers: Auditing Our Own Slope Calculator

Our slope calculator has three input modes now — two points, an equation, a rise/run pair. The third went in on 7 November.

Nobody tested the fraction parser that day. So I pulled our own math.mjs out of the site, 264 lines of exact rational arithmetic, and imported it in Node.

Sixty-seven inputs. Fifty-nine numbers back. Every one matched an independent Python recomputation. Zero arithmetic errors, two display problems.

Method

The calculator page loads app.mjs, which imports math.mjs. That file is pure: no DOM, no canvas, no side effects. It goes into Node 22 with a single import statement, which means the maths can be tested from a terminal instead of by clicking.

import { slopeFromPoints, slopeFromEquation, slopeFromRiseRun } from './math.mjs';

Inputs, chosen to be unkind:

  • 24 point pairs — negative coordinates, both-negative, .5, 5., equal x, identical points
  • 14 proportion setups — one blank cell each, including a zero denominator
  • 17 raw strings — 3/4, 1 1/2, 3/-4, -3/-4, 1e3, 1/0, thirty-three characters
  • 6 Ax + By = C triples
  • 6 rise/run pairs

Python's Fraction recomputed each answer from the same inputs. Two engines, one comparison.

The two things that did fail

Angle drops the sign. m = 2 reports 63.43°. m = −2 also reports 63.43°, while the grade cell beside it reports −200%. Two adjacent stats, opposite meanings, no note anywhere that the angle is unsigned.

Two minus glyphs in one equation. y = -2x − 1 — hyphen before 2x, U+2212 before 1. Nine of twenty-one equations carry the wide minus. Nearly invisible until you compare against a printed textbook.

What this does not cover

The graph. drawGraph() needs a DOM and pointer events, so no browser was opened. Axis scale, plotted position, drag behaviour, and the 375px layout of the steps panel are all unverified. Fifty-nine assertions about arithmetic say nothing about whether the page is usable.

And a note on our own tools: the first two runs produced three false alarms, all from our regex and our test harness, not from the calculator. The accusations were broken before the accused was.
